Split a Group Bill: Shares First, Then Who Pays Whom

Split a bill equally or by weight, and generate settlement suggestions based on what each person has already paid.

Updated, rules checked: 2026-09-16 · How calculations work

Enter the calculation inputs

Check with an example

368 yuan split equally among three people: the first two pay 122.67 yuan each, the third pays 122.66 yuan, and the total is exactly 368 yuan.

If the first person fronted the whole bill, tick the amounts-paid option and enter 368, 0, 0 to see who should transfer money to the first person.

When to use weights

When people took part for different lengths of time or consumed different shares, agree on a ratio first, then enter weights. Weights of 1, 1, 2 for three people mean the third person bears two shares — not 2 yuan. A 368 yuan bill split in that ratio gives 92, 92, 184 yuan. The tool does not judge which split is fair; it only applies the ratio you confirm.

What if someone has already fronted money

First fix the total bill, then enter what everyone actually paid. The amounts paid must add up to the total bill including any tip before settlement is possible. For each person, paid minus owed: a positive value means they should receive money back, a negative value means they need to pay more. The tool matches amounts receivable and payable in the order people are listed and gives a workable set of transfers; it does not guarantee the fewest possible transfers.

Rounding remainders and double charging

Amounts are allocated to the cent: each share is rounded down first, and the remaining cents are distributed by the size of each share's fractional remainder; ties follow the order of the people. If the bill already includes a service charge, enter 0 for the additional percentage. Split results and amounts paid are not saved or uploaded by this tool.

Choose who shares each individual expense

Useful when drinks are split separately, several people front money, or someone joins a trip midway. Each expense is split equally among the people ticked for it; people earlier in the list bear the leftover cents from an equal split. Expenses must already be paid, and all in the same currency.

Fix the list before entering expenses. Updating the list clears the bill below; refreshing the page also clears it, and the tool does not save or upload this data.

Default example: Xiaolin pays 300 yuan for the meal, split among three; Xiaochen pays 68 yuan for drinks, split only between Xiaolin and Xiaochen. Amounts owed are 134, 134, 100 yuan; Xiaochen transfers 66 yuan to Xiaolin, and Xiaozhou transfers 100 yuan to Xiaolin.

This gives one set of transfers that settles the bill; it does not guarantee the fewest transfers. An expense paid by several people can be entered as separate items, with rounding remainders allocated per item.
