Check with an example

1 inch = 2.54 centimeters. 1 US liquid gallon = 3.785411784 liters, while 1 Imperial gallon = 4.54609 liters — the two must not be interchanged.

Pick the category first, then the two units

Units within the same category can be converted into each other. Length and area are different dimensions: for example, 1 meter = 100 centimeters, but 1 square meter = 10,000 square centimeters. Don't apply a length ratio directly to areas.

Conventions used on this page

The jin, li and mu use the Chinese market system: 500 grams, 500 meters, and 2000/3 square meters respectively. Pounds and ounces use the avoirdupois system, not troy. Gallons are explicitly divided into US liquid and Imperial. The page shows at most six decimal places, and also gives more significant figures, so that a very small value shown as 0 is not mistaken for exactly zero.

Temperature is not just a ratio

Celsius to Fahrenheit uses °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; Celsius to Kelvin uses K = °C + 273.15. Temperature conversion accepts negative Celsius values, but nothing below absolute zero. All other categories accept only non-negative numbers. Unit definitions follow the NIST SP 811 guide to units.
