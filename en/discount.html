Original price 299 yuan: Plan A takes 20% off, then another 10% off, then subtracts 10 yuan, so you pay 205.28 yuan; Plan B subtracts 80 yuan directly, so you pay 219 yuan. Plan A costs 13.72 yuan less.

Why the order matters

Original price 100 yuan, 20% off, and 20 yuan off: discount first, then the coupon gives 100 × 0.8 − 20 = 60 yuan; coupon first, then the discount gives (100 − 20) × 0.8 = 64 yuan. Check the store's checkout rules first, then pick the order — don't always put the fixed amount last.

Stacked discounts and the difference from the original price

Taking 20% off and then 10% off is 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72, i.e. you pay 72% of the original price. After the two discounts are combined, the result is rounded to the cent. In discount-first mode the fixed amount is subtracted next; in coupon-first mode the original price is first reduced, but not below 0, and then discounted. Shipping is added at the end. If shipping pushes the amount paid above the original price, the difference from the original price in the table shows as a negative number.

What else to check before ordering

  • Whether the coupon has a minimum spend and applies only to selected items.
  • Whether shipping is charged separately and whether it is discounted.
  • Whether the store rounds at every step; this tool rounds only once, after combining the discounts.
  • Cashback, points, taxes and the value of free gifts are not included automatically.
