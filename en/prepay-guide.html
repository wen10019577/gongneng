Is Prepaying Your Mortgage Worth It? Prepay 100,000 Yuan and Save How Much Interest — the Two Ways Differ by Over 40,000

'How much interest does prepaying 100,000 yuan save?' has no standard answer, because it depends on when you prepay and how the loan is handled afterwards. This guide continues the 1,000,000-yuan, 20-year loan: after 3 years of payments, prepay 100,000 yuan — and shortening the term versus reducing the monthly payment saves interest that differs by over 40,000 yuan.

Written by Ernzi; worked examples re-checked · Updated: 2026-11-04

This guide is a teaching calculation: equal payment, at a fixed assumed rate of 3.6% a year, not representing any bank's pricing or policies. Penalties, frequency limits and minimum amounts vary greatly between banks — go by your loan contract and the handling bank's answers. For the basic comparison of the two repayment methods, see the equal payment vs equal principal guide.

Note the balance at the end of year three: the monthly payments over three years totalled about 210,000 yuan, of which the principal only fell by about 108,000 yuan and the rest was all interest — this is the real-world source of the equal-payment feeling that ‘most of what you pay early on is interest’, and exactly why the earlier you prepay, the more future interest you cut off.

Prepay 100,000 yuan: the two ways compared

Right after period 36, prepay 100000 yuan of principal in one go, bringing the remaining principal to 791783.43 yuan. Banks usually offer two ways of handling it:

With the same 100,000 yuan, shortening the term saves about 42468 yuan more interest than reducing the monthly payment. The reason is the same old line: interest is calculated on the balance. When you shorten the term, the payment stays at 5851 yuan and every extra bit each month chews into principal, so the balance falls noticeably faster than ‘payment down to 5195 yuan, slowly repaying the full 204 periods’. So: if you’re going for interest savings, choose shortening the term; only choose reducing the monthly payment if you’re going for a monthly breather. When you handle it at the bank, proactively confirm which of the two the bank is giving you — the default option isn’t necessarily the one you want.

Three common misunderstandings, corrected first

‘The first years are all interest, so prepaying means you paid for nothing’ — wrong. Each period’s interest is settled on that month’s balance; there is no unpaid interest outstanding. The 102423.56 yuan of interest already paid corresponds to the cost of genuinely occupying over 900,000 yuan of principal during these three years; prepaying only affects interest from now on, and has nothing to do with how much you’ve already paid.

‘Once you’ve repaid a third of an equal-payment loan, prepaying is no longer worth it’ — there is no such line. Whether it’s worth it depends on two numbers only: how much interest the remaining principal will still generate at the current rate (in this guide’s example it’s on the order of 300,000 yuan, and even in year 10 there is considerable room), and whether this idle money has somewhere to go that yields more than the loan rate at a risk you can bear.

‘If you have money you should prepay it all’ — keep a safety cushion. A mortgage is one of the lowest-rate loans an ordinary person can get. Before prepaying, first set aside 6–12 months of household expenses as an emergency fund, then clear higher-rate consumer loans and credit-card instalments, and only then does the mortgage’s turn come. Putting all your cash into the mortgage and then borrowing at high interest when an emergency hits is getting the order wrong.

Checklist before you act (tick off against the contract, item by item)

  1. Penalty or handling fee: some contracts charge a percentage of the remaining principal for prepaying before a certain number of years — first work out whether it would eat the interest you save.
  2. Frequency and minimum amount: how many times a year you may do it, and the minimum per time, differ between banks.
  3. Whether an appointment is needed: branches in busy cities may queue for weeks — count the time cost in.
  4. Handling method: shortening the term or reducing the monthly payment — confirm on the spot and keep the receipt; for a floating-rate loan, also confirm how the payment changes on the repricing date.
  5. Housing fund loans and combined loans: the rules differ for the commercial part and the housing fund part; for a combined loan it’s generally more worthwhile to repay the commercial part first — confirm the order with the bank before you act.

FAQ

Where does the interest saved by prepaying come from?

What you prepay is principal. From then on, every period’s interest is calculated on the lower remaining principal; what you save is all the interest that principal would have generated over the remaining term. The earlier you repay and the longer the remaining term, the more you save.

Shorten the term or reduce the monthly payment — which to choose?

If the goal is saving interest, choose shortening the term. In this guide’s example, prepaying 100,000 yuan saves about 76315 yuan of interest by shortening the term and about 33847 yuan by reducing the monthly payment — a difference of about 42468 yuan. Only choose reducing the monthly payment if the goal is lowering monthly pressure.

People say prepaying after several years isn’t worth it — is that true?

It’s a misunderstanding. Each period’s interest is settled on that period’s balance; there is no ‘the interest has already been paid off’. Whether it’s worth it depends only on how much interest the remaining principal will still generate and whether you have a better use for the money — not on how many years you’ve repaid (though the later you prepay, the less there is to save).

What should I confirm before prepaying?

Four things in the contract: penalty, how many times a year, the minimum amount each time, and whether an appointment is needed. Then confirm whether the bank’s default handling after repayment is shortening the term or reducing the monthly payment — if it doesn’t match your goal, ask to change it on the spot.
