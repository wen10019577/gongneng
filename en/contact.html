Contact and corrections

If you have a question about a calculation result, or want to feed back a real-life scenario, you can contact the maintainer by email.

Clicking the link will try to open your device's mail app; you can also copy the address and send from your usual email service. This site has no online customer service and makes no promise of an immediate response.

For calculation questions, please include the following

  1. The URL or name of the tool page.
  2. The values, units and mode you selected.
  3. The actual result and the result you expected.
  4. If you have a basis, the formula, your hand calculation, or a link to a public source.

You can reproduce the issue with fictional example amounts. Do not send ID numbers, bank card numbers, complete bills, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Content or privacy feedback

If you believe information on a page is out of date, a source is not clearly attributed, or you have questions about how this site handles data, you can also use the same email address. Email is received and processed by the maintainer through Gmail, and the information you provide is used to communicate about and handle the issue.
