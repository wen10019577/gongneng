Calculation and Maintenance Notes

Learn about the precision of the results, the scope of testing, and what substantive changes this version made.

Updated: 2026-09-16

Calculation conventions

Amounts in bill splitting and shopping are handled in cents; bill splitting uses largest-remainder allocation, so that independent rounding for each person cannot make the totals disagree. Loans and compound interest are mathematical models: they keep more precision internally and round only for display, so they are not equivalent to an institution's bill.

Dates are calculated as Gregorian calendar days. Numeric inputs are checked for finiteness, range or integer conditions; blank or invalid input should produce a prompt, rather than silently defaulting to zero or continuing the calculation with only part of the input.

Changes in the 2026-09-16 version

  • Added comparison of two discount offers, with support for shipping fees and the order in which discounts apply.
  • Bill splitting gained payment reconciliation and settlement suggestions between people.
  • Mortgage calculations support a zero interest rate, a complete repayment schedule and CSV export.
  • Pay is calculated from a self-entered personal contribution amount, showing how cumulative withholding changes under a fixed-salary scenario.
  • The age page removed the fixed retirement age and the simplified nominal-age explanation, and states the leap-day birthday convention explicitly.
  • Base conversion uses strict character validation and big-integer arithmetic.
  • All tools gained recomputable examples and a statement of scope.

Testing and limitations

This version's checks cover known input/output pairs, invalid inputs, key boundaries, conservation of the total when splitting bills, and page form interactions. Automated checks cannot stand in for every real-world business scenario, and the tools hold no certification from an independent body. The tax and BMI pages provide public reference sources, but they do not handle every condition of an individual case.

If you find a result that does not match its explanation, please provide the details needed to reproduce it via the contact page. The update date records when this site's version was maintained; it does not mean external rules necessarily changed on that day.
