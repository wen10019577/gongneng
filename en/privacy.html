Privacy Notice

How calculation inputs, third-party advertising, site visits and email feedback are handled.

Updated: 2026-09-16

Calculations in your browser

In this version, the calculation code runs in your browser. It does not send your calculation inputs to this site's server, does not save inputs to local storage, and does not put input values into links. Results may be lost when you refresh or close the page. When you download a CSV, the file is saved to your device and is yours to manage.

Advertising and network requests

Tool pages and the home page load Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, IP addresses or other identifiers to process ad requests, measure performance and prevent abuse, depending on your region, consent settings and browser restrictions. See How Google uses information from partner sites and Google's advertising technologies.

The site is served through Cloudflare infrastructure, whose services may process IP addresses, request paths and security logs. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. This notice does not promise that third-party services process no data at all.

Managing your choices

You can manage cookies and site data in your browser, and adjust applicable advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center. If a page displays a consent management message, you can use the options it provides to manage your choices. Blocking advertising services does not affect the calculation logic of this version.

Email and external links

When you send a feedback email, the maintainer receives your email address and the content of your email, which are used to handle the issue. Please provide only necessary information. Services at external links are governed by their own privacy policies. For questions about this notice, contact wenyangyang599@gmail.com.
