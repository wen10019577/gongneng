Geometric area: unify the dimensions first, then calculate

Calculate the area of rectangles, circles, triangles and trapezoids. The fields change with the shape; all dimensions are entered in meters.

Updated and rules checked: 2026-09-16 · Calculation notes

Enter the inputs

Check with an example

A room 4 meters long and 3 meters wide has a rectangular area of 12 square meters. For a circle, the input is the radius; for a diameter of 4 meters you should enter a radius of 2 meters.

Formulas for each shape

  • Rectangle: length × width.
  • Circle: π × radius².
  • Triangle: base × vertical height ÷ 2.
  • Trapezoid: (top base + bottom base) × vertical height ÷ 2.

Deal with units and vertical height first

Convert 300 centimeters to 3 meters before entering it. A triangle's slanted side is not the height relative to its base, and a trapezoid's two non-parallel sides are not the height by default either. If you only know a triangle's three sides, you cannot simply enter two of them as the base and the height.

One more step from area to purchase quantity

Tiling, painting or cutting fabric also requires allowing for waste, deducting doors and windows, the coverage area per pack, and how the work is done. Area is a geometric value, not the final purchase quantity. Results are shown to at most six decimal places; do not mistake display precision for measurement precision.
