Age in full years: how old on a given date?

Calculate completed years of age from a date of birth and a reference date, together with the number of days between them and the days until the next birthday.

Born on September 20, 2000: on September 16, 2026, the person is 25 years old; on September 20, the birthday itself, they turn 26.

Age is not just the difference of the years

First subtract the birth year from the reference year, then check whether this year's birthday has already passed. If it has not, subtract 1 more. This tool reports only completed whole years of age, avoiding "negative days" results caused by borrowing across months of different lengths.

Born on February 29?

This tool uses an explicit convention: in non-leap years, the birthday anniversary falls on March 1. So someone born on February 29, 2024 is still 0 years old on February 28, 2025, and turns 1 on March 1. This is a calculation convention of this tool; it does not replace the age rules of any specific official process.

What this tool does not provide

It does not estimate retirement dates, school eligibility, or other legal qualifications, and it does not treat "age plus one" as an accurate traditional nominal age. Such questions can depend on the birth date, local rules, the lunar calendar, or special definitions. On the birthday itself, "days until the next birthday" shows 0 days.
