Terms of Use and Disclaimer

Before using this site's calculators and guide articles, please read this page in full: what this site provides are reproducible estimates and explanations of methods — not official results from any institution, and not professional advice.

Updated: 2026-11-04

What this site provides

Ernzi provides everyday calculators (shopping discounts, bill splitting, dates, pay and tax estimates, loans and interest, electricity use, conversions, and more) together with matching guide articles on how the calculations work. The tools run in your browser. The worked examples in the guide articles use numbers constructed for teaching, with their assumptions stated, so readers can recompute them step by step and then substitute their own data.

Nature and limits of the results

  • Estimates: every calculation result is an estimate produced under the model and assumptions stated on its page. External rules such as tax rates, social insurance contribution bases, loan interest rates and electricity prices change, and they also vary by region and by personal circumstances. This site marks the date each page was checked, but does not guarantee that a rule is still in force when you use it.
  • Official sources and contracts prevail: for individual income tax, the tax authority's withholding and annual settlement results prevail; for loans, the bank contract and repayment schedule prevail; for electricity charges, the power supply company's billing rules and bills prevail; for health indicators such as BMI, a professional assessment by a medical institution prevails.
  • Rounding and definitional differences: this site calculates by the conventions explained on each page and rounds the results for display. Institutions may accrue interest daily or round item by item, which can produce small differences; differences of this kind are not calculation errors.
