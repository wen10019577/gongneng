China Annual Tax on Comprehensive Income: Income Amount and Prepaid Difference

Enter your converted annual comprehensive income and deductions to estimate the year's tax, and compare it with the tax already prepaid.

Updated · Rules checked: 2026-09-16 · How calculations work

Check against an example

Income amount 120,000 yuan, other deductions 12,000 yuan: taxable income is 48,000 yuan, and the tax is 2,280 yuan. If 2,280 yuan was also prepaid, the difference is 0.

First, distinguish income from income amount

The income amount is not computed the same way for wages and salaries, remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration and royalties. This tool asks for the annual income amount already converted under the applicable rules — you cannot simply add up bank transfers. The tool does not classify income types, and it does not handle business income or separately taxed bonuses.

How to enter deductions

The basic deduction of 60,000 yuan is applied automatically by the tool; do not enter it again under other deductions. Other deductions should total the actual amounts that qualify under the rules. Deductions only affect taxable income; they are not all amounts paid out of your cash income.

Basis and scope

Estimated using the annual rate table for residents' comprehensive income: tax equals taxable income multiplied by the applicable rate, minus the quick deduction. Basis: the Individual Income Tax Law and its schedules (State Taxation Administration); this page was checked on 2026-09-16. Results do not cover tax reductions and exemptions, foreign tax credits, donation caps, or any assessment of exemption from filing a reconciliation; your actual filing follows the tax authorities' calculation.

Another example, one income band higher

With annual comprehensive income of 300000 yuan and other deductions of 36000 yuan: taxable income = 300000 − 60000 − 36000 = 204000 yuan, which falls in the 20% bracket, so tax = 204000 × 20% − 16920 = 23880 yuan. Note that the quick deduction is used only once, inside the quick formula that applies a single rate to the whole cumulative amount — it is never deducted repeatedly, bracket by bracket. Treat it as a correction term, not as yet another deduction.

What does a negative prepaid difference mean?

Finally, the tool compares the estimated tax with the amount you prepaid: a positive difference is tax you may have to pay at reconciliation; a negative difference is tax that may be refunded. This is only an estimate — tax reductions, foreign credits, donation deductions and how a year-end bonus is taxed can all change the final result, and some situations are exempt from filing a reconciliation at all. For your formal filing, follow the tax authorities' system.

Frequently asked questions

Is the income amount the same as my bank transfers? No. Income amounts for remuneration for personal services, author's remuneration and similar income each have their own conversion rules (author's remuneration, for instance, is counted at 70% after its conversion), so adding up transfers directly would overstate them.

Why don't I enter the basic deduction myself? The 60000 yuan is deducted automatically by the tool. Entering it again under "other deductions" would deduct it twice and understate your tax.

Where do I calculate monthly withholding on wages? Use the take-home pay calculator; this page takes the whole-year view, and using both shows why withholding and reconciliation don't line up. The derivation is in the cumulative withholding method, explained.
